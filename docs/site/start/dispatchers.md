# Dispatcher tasks

> **Audience:** staff — you own the schedule: what gets worked today, who
> drives where, and whether next week has the hours to carry it.

## "The queue has work nobody owns"

1. [Dispatch from the board](../how-to/staff-dispatch.md) — read the roster, drag
   unassigned tickets onto open slots, and clear the conflicts the board raises.
2. [Plan the week ahead](../how-to/staff-dispatch-planning.md) — the read-only
   horizon: demand by priority against roster hours, the skill gaps, and the
   project load already promised.
3. [Understand role permissions](../how-to/admin-roles-and-permissions.md) — what
   the dispatcher role can change, and which settings need an admin.

## "A technician is running late"

- [Run a service day from the phone](../how-to/technician-service-day.md) — the
  technician's side of the same day: accept the visit, clock in, log the drive.
- [Work a day pack](../how-to/staff-day-pack.md) — what has to be in the van
  before the route leaves.
- [Use multiple windows](../how-to/staff-multi-window.md) — the board, stock
  lookup and a ticket open at once, across two monitors.

## "Travel and mileage need settling"

- [Log travel and reconcile mileage](../how-to/staff-dispatch-travel.md) — read
  the 14-day reconciliation, check for duplicate trips, and turn drafts into time
  entries and expenses.
- [Turn on travel time and mileage reconciliation](../how-to/admin-routing-travel.md) —
  the org settings behind those numbers: routing provider, day start, mileage
  rate and whether technicians are reimbursed.

## "A client should book their own slot"

- [Set up meeting types and scheduling groups](../how-to/staff-dispatch-scheduling.md) —
  duration, buffer, booking window, the pool a link draws from, and the email
  that fires with it.
- [Set up the portal](../how-to/admin-portal-setup.md) — the account a client
  signs in to beside a booking link.

## "I am stuck"

- [Get help inside Marula](../how-to/get-help.md) — the `?` panel on the surface
  you are on.
- [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md) — start from what you see, not from
  which page owns it.

## Not documented yet

- **Auto-assign, end to end.** The board footer shows the indicator, and it stays
  off unless an admin enables routing for a scheduling group. No page walks
  through enabling it, the tickets it moves, or how to undo a run.
- **The scheduling link lifecycle on a ticket.** Sending, resending and
  cancelling a link happens in the ticket's scheduling panel. The admin side is
  documented; the ticket side is not.
