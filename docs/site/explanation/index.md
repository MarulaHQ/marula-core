# Explanation

Explanation is understanding-oriented: it discusses why things are the way
they are, connects ideas, and lets you form your own conclusions. It does
not teach a procedure (that is [tutorials](../tutorials/index.md)) or list
facts for lookup (that is [reference](../reference/index.md)).

Every explanation page states its **Audience** up front — site operator /
MSP admin, MSP staff, or both.

- [Why we're building Meridian](why-meridian.md) — the canonical positioning
  narrative, quoted from `MANIFESTO.md` verbatim. Audience: both.
- [Provenance vs audit](provenance-vs-audit.md) — two append-only ledgers,
  field-level vs event-level, and why both never rewrite history.
  Audience: both.
- [FX additivity](fx-additivity.md) — one base currency, an append-only
  rate book, and why reconciliation totals are per kind and never summed
  across kinds. Audience: both.
- [AI: deny-by-default and the decision harness](ai-deny-by-default.md) —
  the master switch semantics, typed decisions, and the guarantee that
  OFF is the pre-AI product exactly. Audience: both.
- [KB-class categories and the secret vault](kb-class-categories.md) —
  category-bound grants, hidden-not-dead, audited reveals (ADR 0006).
  Audience: both.
- [The portal isolation model](portal-isolation.md) — per-client trust
  zones, structural search bars, provisioning-owned credentials.
  Audience: both.
- [Instant-first interaction](instant-first.md) — the binding interaction
  doctrine and its support contract. Audience: both.
- [Contract relationship types](contract-relationship-types.md) — the six
  MSP-client agreement shapes Meridian bills, the configuration that
  rides them (prepaid blocks, NTE caps, minimums, escalators, change
  orders, SLA credits), and why tax stays at the accounting-export
  boundary. Audience: both.
- [Customizations are data](customizations-are-data.md) — why declarative
  rows make upgrades boring. Audience: both.
- [The upgrade compatibility policy](upgrade-policy.md) — bounded promises,
  mechanically enforced. Audience: admin.
