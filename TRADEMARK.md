# Trademark policy

The **Marula** name, the Marula wordmark and the Marula logo are trademarks of the
project's owner. This policy covers those marks only. It does not change your rights
under the code licences.

## The short version

The AGPL-3.0 gives you the right to use, modify and redistribute the **code**. It
gives you no right to use the **name or logo** in a way that suggests your fork is the
project, or that the project endorses it.

## You may, without asking

- **State the facts:** "this is a fork of Marula Core", "based on Marula Core", "built
  on Marula". Factual, descriptive references are fine.
- **Redistribute unmodified copies** under the AGPL-3.0, keeping the name and
  attribution notices intact.
- **Use the name in writing about the software** — reviews, tutorials, comparisons,
  conference talks, course material.
- **Link to this repository** and use the name in a package or module path where the
  AGPL requires the notice to travel with the code.

## You must ask first

- **Naming a fork or a derivative product "Marula"**, or anything confusingly similar,
  as its primary name. A modified version you distribute publicly should carry its own
  name, with "based on Marula Core" as attribution.
- **Using the logo** in a product, an app icon, a store listing, or marketing for your
  own service.
- **Suggesting endorsement or affiliation** — "Marula Certified", "Marula Official",
  "powered by Marula" used as a brand claim rather than a factual description.
- **Registering domains, social handles or company names** that incorporate the mark.

Hosting Marula Core for third parties is permitted by the AGPL and this policy does not
restrict it; presenting that service *as* Marula is what needs permission.

## Logo and brand assets

The logo files distributed in this repository are provided so the software can display
its own identity. They are not a brand kit, and this policy grants no right to reuse
them outside that purpose. Brand questions: **`oss@getmarula.com`**.
